# Code of conduct

RaceLab welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities and levels of experience. This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests and other project spaces on GitHub, including maintainers' participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and discuss the work with specific, constructive feedback.
- Make room for questions, different perspectives and honest mistakes.
- Credit other people's contributions and respect their privacy.
- Disagree with technical claims using evidence rather than personal attacks.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual advances, doxxing, spam and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not publish someone's private information without permission.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's **Report content** action for abusive or harmful GitHub content. GitHub's [reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) explains the available reporting channels. Do not repeat sensitive details in a public issue.

For a concern that is safe to discuss publicly, open a repository issue with links to the relevant interaction and the behavior that needs attention. Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may ask participants to change their behavior, edit or remove disruptive contributions, close or lock discussions, or restrict participation using GitHub's moderation tools. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior. These expectations apply equally to maintainers.
